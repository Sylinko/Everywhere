## Permitted Referential Use

You may refer to **Sylinko** and **Everywhere** for accurate, non-misleading factual reference—for example:

- Describing compatibility with Everywhere (e.g., *"MyPlugin for Everywhere"* or *"Compatible with Everywhere"*);
- Linking to the official repository (`https://github.com/Sylinko/Everywhere`) or official website (`https://everywhere.sylinko.com`);
- Preserving required copyright and attribution notices when redistributing source code under the applicable license.

## Prohibited Uses

Without prior written permission from Sylinko Inc., you must not:

- Redistribute or reuse any of the reserved Sylinko visual artwork listed above (or confusingly similar derivatives) in a modified build, fork, or third-party product;
- Distribute a modified version or fork of Everywhere under the name **Sylinko**, or under any name, icon, or presentation likely to confuse users into believing it is an official Sylinko release of Everywhere;
- Use the `everywhere.*` or `sylinko.*` mod identifier prefixes for third-party mods, packages, or unofficial repositories;
- Present any third-party build, service, or mod in a manner that states or implies official origin, sponsorship, certification, or endorsement by Sylinko Inc.

When distributing a modified build or fork of Everywhere under `AGPL-3.0-only`, you must replace the reserved artwork listed above and choose a distinct application name and identifier so users can clearly distinguish your distribution from official Sylinko releases.
